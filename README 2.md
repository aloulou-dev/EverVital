# EverVital

EverVital is a health and longevity companion that helps you understand and improve your well‑being. It combines your lifestyle inputs and Apple Health data to estimate a realistic life‑expectancy range, provide a personalized health score, and deliver clear, quantified recommendations you can act on. With privacy‑first design and on‑device experiences, EverVital gives you an approachable way to track progress and make informed choices that may extend both lifespan and healthspan.
